FinancePro

Welcome to your ledger.

FinancePro doesn't connect to your bank — it's a place you log money by hand, the moment it moves. Get paid, spend on food, send a transfer: add it here as an entry.

Over time that builds a real picture: your balance, where money actually goes each month, whether you're within budget, and how close you are to what you're saving for.

Everything is stored only on this device. Nobody else can see it — but that also means it's worth exporting a backup now and then (Settings → Export).

Transactions

Every entry in the book, searchable and sortable.

Log every entry the moment money moves — income or expense, with an amount, category, and date. Use Quick Add below for things you log often, so you don't retype them each time. Budgets

Set a limit per category, watch it as the month goes.

Set a monthly spending limit for a category — say ₦20,000 for Food. As you log expense entries in that category this month, the bar fills up. Cross the limit and it's flagged as over budget. It resets naturally each month since it only counts the current month's spending.

Savings Goals

Name what you're saving for. Watch it get closer.

Goals aren't pulled from your transactions automatically — they're separate and manual. Name something you're saving toward, set a target, then tap "Add funds" whenever you set money aside for it. Good for tracking progress on something specific, like an emergency fund or a big purchase.
